# GharKhoj User Guide

Welcome to **GharKhoj**, the easy way to find and list rooms, flats, apartments, houses and office spaces for rent across Nepal.

This guide is for everyone who uses the website:

- [Part 1 — Renters: find a place and book a visit](#part-1--renters)
- [Part 2 — Landlords: list your property and manage visits](#part-2--landlords)
- [Part 3 — Administrators: keep the marketplace safe](#part-3--administrators)
- [Part 4 — Safety tips, privacy and FAQ](#part-4--safety-privacy-and-faq)

GharKhoj works on any phone, tablet or computer with a web browser. No app download is needed.

---

## Part 1 — Renters

### 1.1 Search without an account

You can browse everything without signing in.

1. Open the home page and type a **city, neighbourhood or landmark** (for example _Baneshwor_, _Lakeside_, _near Bhatbhateni_), choose a city if you like, and tap **Search**.
2. Or tap a city under **Popular cities**.

### 1.2 Narrow your results with filters

On a phone, tap **Filters**; on a computer the filters are on the left.

| Filter               | What it does                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Keyword              | Matches the title, landmark and description                                                                                    |
| City / Neighbourhood | Limits results to an area                                                                                                      |
| Property type        | Room, Studio/Flat, Apartment, House, Office/Studio space                                                                       |
| Monthly rent (NPR)   | Type a minimum and maximum, or tap a quick range like _10K–25K_                                                                |
| Bedrooms / Bathrooms | _2+_ means two or more                                                                                                         |
| Furnishing           | Unfurnished, semi-furnished, fully furnished                                                                                   |
| Tenant type accepted | Family, bachelors, students, couples, working professionals                                                                    |
| Must have            | Pet-friendly, parking, Wi-Fi, 24h water, electricity backup, hot water and more. A place must have **all** the items you tick. |
| Move in by           | Shows places available on or before your date                                                                                  |
| Area                 | Minimum/maximum size in square feet                                                                                            |

Your filters are saved in the page address, so you can **bookmark or share the link** and the same search opens for whoever you send it to. Tap **Clear all filters** to start again.

**Sort** results by _Newest_, _Price: low to high_, _Price: high to low_ or _Most relevant_.

### 1.3 Search on the map

1. Tap **Map** above the results.
2. Each bubble shows the monthly rent (_25K_ = Rs. 25,000; _1.5L_ = Rs. 1,50,000). Numbers in coloured circles are groups of places; zoom in to separate them.
3. Tap a bubble to see a photo and price, then tap it again to open the listing.
4. Move or zoom the map, then tap **Search this area** to show only places in view.
5. Tap **Within 2 km of centre** to search around the middle of the map, or **Near me** to search near your current location (your browser will ask permission).

> Some landlords show only an **approximate area** for privacy. Those places appear as a shaded circle on the listing's map; the exact address is shared once your visit is confirmed.

### 1.4 Read a listing

Each listing page shows:

- **Photos.** Swipe or use the arrows; tap a photo for full screen and pinch to zoom.
- **Price.** Monthly rent, deposit, minimum lease and whether the rent is negotiable.
- **Key facts.** Bedrooms, bathrooms, area, furnishing, floor and availability date.
- **Utilities.** Water supply (tanker, boring, municipal), power backup, cooking gas, internet, waste collection, parking.
- **House rules.** Who the landlord prefers (family, students, and so on), pets and smoking.
- **Amenities** and **floor plans**.
- **Location map.**
- **Listed by.** The landlord's name, the year they joined, their response rate, and a ✔ **Verified** badge if GharKhoj has checked their identity documents.

Use **Share** to send the listing on WhatsApp or Facebook, or to copy the link.

### 1.5 Create an account

You need an account to save favourites and request visits.

1. Tap **Sign in** (top right) → **Create renter account**.
2. Enter your name, email and a password, then type the code we email you.

### 1.6 Save favourites ❤

Tap the **heart** on any listing card or page. Find your saved places under the heart icon at the top, or **Account → Favourites**. Tap the heart again to remove one.

### 1.7 Save a search

On a results page, tap **Save search**, give it a name (for example _2BHK under 30K in Lalitpur_) and choose how often you'd like updates. Saved searches are listed under **Account → Saved searches**.

### 1.8 Request a visit (tour)

1. On the listing page, use the **Schedule a tour** panel (on phones, tap **Schedule a tour** at the bottom of the screen).
2. **Pick a day.** Only days when the landlord is available can be selected.
3. **Choose up to 3 times** that suit you. All times are **Nepal time**.
4. Enter your **mobile number** (for example `98XXXXXXXX`) and, optionally, a short message such as who will live there and when you want to move in.
5. Tap **Request tour**.

What happens next:

| Status                | Meaning                                                                                               |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Awaiting landlord** | The landlord has been emailed and will pick one of your times                                         |
| **Confirmed**         | You get an email with the time, the landlord's phone number and the address. Tap **Add to calendar**. |
| **New time proposed** | The landlord suggested a different time. Tap **Accept new time** or **Cancel tour**.                  |
| **Declined**          | The landlord can't host you; their note, if any, is shown                                             |

You'll get **reminder emails** 24 hours and 2 hours before a confirmed visit. See all your visits under **Account → Tours**.

> You can have **one open request per listing**. Cancel it if your plans change.

### 1.9 Report a listing

If something looks wrong (a scam, a fake photo, or a place that's already rented), tap **Report listing** at the bottom of the page, choose a reason and add details. Our team reviews every report.

## Part 2 — Landlords

### 2.1 Become a landlord

- **New users:** Sign in → **I'm a landlord** → create your account.
- **Existing renters:** **Account → Become a landlord → Switch my account to landlord**.

Listing on GharKhoj is free.

### 2.2 Create a listing (about 5–10 minutes)

Go to **Landlord dashboard → New listing** (or **List your property** in the header) and fill in each section:

1. **Basics.** A clear title (for example _Sunny 2BHK flat with parking near Baneshwor chowk_), the property type, the furnishing and a description. Mention water, sunlight, road access, nearby landmarks and house rules.
2. **Price & lease.** Monthly rent in NPR, security deposit (usually 1–3 months' rent), available-from date, minimum lease, and whether the price is negotiable.
3. **Details.** Bedrooms (0 for a studio or office), bathrooms, area in sq ft, floor number and total floors (0 = ground floor), parking spots, facing direction, year built.
4. **Utilities.** Water supply, electricity backup, cooking gas, internet, waste collection. Renters look at these first.
5. **Who can rent.** Your preferred tenant types, and whether pets or smoking are allowed. These are shown openly to renters.
6. **Location.**
   - Choose the **city** and **neighbourhood/tole**, then add the **ward number**, a **landmark** (for example _200 m north of Bhatbhateni_) and the street/tole address.
   - **Place the map pin.** Tap the map where the property is, or search a landmark and pick a result. **Drag the pin** to adjust it.
   - Keep **Show approximate location only** ticked if you don't want your exact house shown publicly. Renters will see a shaded circle, and your exact address is shared only with renters whose visit you confirm.
7. **Amenities.** Tick everything that applies.

Tap **Save draft & continue**. Your listing is saved as a **Draft**, which only you can see.

### 2.3 Add photos and floor plans

On the edit page, in the **Photos** section:

- **Click to choose** or **drag and drop** photos (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each, up to 25 photos). You'll see upload progress.
- Location data hidden inside phone photos (GPS) is **removed automatically**.
- **Reorder** by dragging, or with the ↑ ↓ buttons. The photo marked **Cover** appears in search results; use **Make cover** to change it.
- Choose a **room label** (Bedroom, Kitchen, …) and add an optional caption, then tap **Save photo order & captions**.
- **Floor plans** (optional): tap **Add floor plan** (JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 15 MB).

Good photos get more visits: use daylight, take wide shots of every room, and include the view and the water/power setup.

### 2.4 Submit for review

Tap **Submit for review** on the edit page. You need **at least one photo** and a **map pin**; if anything is missing, the page tells you what to fix.

Our team checks every new listing, usually within a few hours:

- **Approved:** you get an email and the listing goes **live**.
- **Changes needed:** you get an email with the reason, and it is also shown at the top of the edit page. Fix the issue and submit again.

> Avoid putting phone numbers, payment instructions or "pay deposit first" in descriptions. Listings like that are usually rejected to protect renters.

If you later change the **title or description** of a live listing, it goes back for a quick review. Changing the price, photos or other details does not.

### 2.5 Manage your listings

**Landlord dashboard → My listings** shows each listing with its status, views, saves and tour requests. Open the **⋯** menu to:

| Action                 | Use it when                                                                      |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Edit                   | Update details or photos                                                         |
| Pause listing / Resume | Temporarily hide the listing from search (for example while repairs are done)    |
| Mark as rented         | Found a tenant; the listing leaves search                                        |
| Relist                 | Available again; it returns to draft, so submit it again                         |
| Archive                | You no longer rent this property                                                 |
| Duplicate              | Create a similar listing quickly (for example another flat in the same building) |
| Tour availability      | Set when renters can visit                                                       |
| Delete                 | Permanently remove the listing and its photos                                    |

### 2.6 Set when renters can visit

**⋯ → Tour availability:**

- **Weekly hours:** add windows such as _Saturday 10:00–16:00_. Renters can only choose 30-minute slots inside them.
- **Unavailable dates:** block festivals (Dashain, Tihar), travel or busy days.
- If you set nothing, renters can choose times between **9 AM and 6 PM every day**.

### 2.7 Respond to tour requests

You get an email for each request. Go to **Tour requests** (from the account menu or the dashboard):

- **Confirm** one of the renter's times. You and the renter both get a confirmation email, and the renter receives your phone number and the address.
- **Propose another time** if none of their times work.
- **Decline** (optionally with a note).
- After the visit, mark it **Completed** or **No-show**.

The renter's name and phone number are shown on each request. Respond quickly, because renters see your **response rate**.

### 2.8 Get the Verified badge ✔

**Dashboard → Verify now:** upload a clear photo or scan of your **citizenship certificate (nagarikta)** or **land ownership document (lalpurja)**. It is stored privately, seen only by our review team, and never shown to renters. You'll get an email with the decision.

### 2.9 Your dashboard

Shows your total listings, total views, how many renters saved your places, and tour requests waiting for you.

## Part 3 — Administrators

Admins see **Admin** in the account menu.

| Page                 | What to do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Overview**         | Counts of users, listings and tours by status; anything needing attention is highlighted                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Moderation queue** | Pending listings, oldest first. Check photos and description. **Warning flags** highlight phone numbers, emails, links, "pay deposit first"/eSewa/WhatsApp cues, failed image checks and possible duplicates. **Approve & publish**, or **Reject…** with a reason (quick reasons available). The landlord is emailed either way. |
| **Reports**          | User reports. **Take down listing** (removes it from search immediately), **Resolve (keep live)**, or **Dismiss**. Add a resolution note.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Verifications**    | Open the landlord's document (a private link valid for 5 minutes; each view is recorded), compare it with the account name, then **Verify** or **Reject** with a note.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Users**            | Search by name or email, change roles, **Ban/Unban**. Banning immediately pauses that landlord's live listings. You can't ban yourself or another admin.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Lookup data**      | Add cities (with map coordinates), neighbourhoods/toles and amenities; switch cities on or off                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Audit log**        | A permanent record of every moderation and admin action, with who did it and when (Nepal time)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

Good practice: clear the queue at least twice a day, act on scam reports first, and leave clear rejection reasons so landlords can fix their listings.

## Part 4 — Safety, privacy and FAQ

### Stay safe

- **Never pay a deposit or "booking fee" before you have visited the property** and met the landlord.
- Be careful if someone asks you to pay through eSewa/Khalti or to move the conversation to WhatsApp before a visit.
- Look for the ✔ **Verified** badge.
- Report anything suspicious. It takes 30 seconds.

### Your privacy

- We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.
- "Recently viewed" is stored only on your own device.
- Your phone number is shared only with the landlord of a property you request to visit.
- You can **delete your account and all your data** at any time: **Account → Delete my account**.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Terms of Service](/terms).

### FAQ

**I didn't get an email.** Check your spam/promotions folder. Emails come from the GharKhoj sender address.

**The map pin is not exactly on the house.** The landlord chose an approximate location. You'll get the exact address when your visit is confirmed.

**Why can't I pick a certain time?** The landlord isn't available then, it is less than 2 hours away, or someone else already booked it.

**Can I list a property without photos?** You can save a draft, but you need at least one photo to submit it.

**How are prices shown?** In Nepali Rupees with Nepali digit grouping: Rs. 1,50,000 is one lakh fifty thousand. On the map, _K_ = thousand and _L_ = lakh.

**Is GharKhoj available in Nepali?** English for now; नेपाली is planned.

Need more help? See the [Help & safety](/help) page.
